Quality control

The refineries we work with run a laboratory that controls sugar from raw material intake to packaging. Analyses follow ICUMSA methods (International Commission for Uniform Methods of Sugar Analysis).

Polarization
Content of pure sucrose in the sugar.
Moisture
One of the key safety tests for white refined sugar.
Conductivity ash
A quick measure of ionic impurities in solution.
Colour
UV/Vis photometer, standard ICUMSA method.
Turbidity
Key for beverage and food manufacturers.
Invert sugar
Glucose and fructose split from sucrose.

A microbiological laboratory checks product and process water for bacteria against international limits.

How a transaction works

The procedure protects both sides: the buyer sees proof of product before the bank instrument becomes operative, and the seller has confirmed funding.

Payment: 100% at sight against shipping documents by MT103, under a DLC (MT700) or SBLC (MT760). Shipment starts within 20-40 days of the operative instrument.

  1. Buyer sends an LOI or ICPO with bank details.
  2. We issue a Full Corporate Offer (FCO); the buyer signs and returns it.
  3. Draft contract, signatures of both parties, contract lodged with both banks.
  4. Proforma invoice.
  5. Buyer's bank: MT799, then MT760 (SBLC) after the seller's bank RWA.
  6. Seller's bank issues proof of product (POP) and a 2% performance bond.
  7. Loading with SGS inspection, full document set, payment by MT103.
